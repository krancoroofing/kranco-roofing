Most Roof Problems Start Small — and Grow Quietly

A hairline crack along a shingle, a joint that has opened a fraction of an inch, flashing that has lifted around a vent pipe — none of these look serious from the ground. But Oklahoma weather works on every weak point: wind drives rain under lifted edges, summer heat bakes sealants until they split, and water that finds a way in travels along the deck until it shows up as a ceiling stain rooms away from the actual entry point.

Kranco Roofing focuses on roof repair Guthrie OK homeowners can call before a small defect turns into rotted decking and interior damage. Every job starts with a free inspection that finds where the water is actually getting in — because fixing the stain's location rarely fixes the leak.

Our Roof Repair Services

Roof Crack & Joint Repair

Hairline fractures, structural cracks and open expansion joints are sealed with polymer-based compounds that flex with the roof instead of splitting again at the next temperature swing.

Leakage & Seepage Treatment

Active leaks around flashing, vents, pipe penetrations and roof corners are traced to their entry point and repaired — not just patched where the water happens to drip.

Roof Waterproofing Treatment

Multi-layer liquid membranes and elastomeric coatings that block rainwater and protect the roof surface from standing water and driven rain.

Roof Heat Proofing

Solar-reflective, UV-resistant coatings that lower roof surface temperatures, ease the load on cooling systems and slow heat-driven aging of the roofing material.

Metal & Industrial Shed Repair

Loose fasteners, seam gaps, rust spots and leaks on metal roofs and industrial sheds — repaired and sealed so the structure stays weather-tight.

Surface Preparation & Restoration

Cleaning, grinding and restoring damaged or hollow surface layers before coating or repair, so new material bonds to a sound surface and lasts.

Signs Your Roof Needs Repair

  • Water stains on ceilings or walls, especially after storms
  • Drips or damp patches in the attic
  • Cracked, curled or missing shingles
  • Daylight visible through the roof boards in the attic
  • Granules collecting in gutters
  • Rust spots, loose seams or rattling panels on a metal roof

Caught early, most of these are repairs. Left through another storm season, they become decking replacement and interior work.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does roof repair cost in Guthrie?

It depends on the damage: sealing cracks and joints is a smaller job than repairing rotted decking around a long-running leak. Kranco Roofing offers free inspections and written quotes, so you know the cost before any work starts. Call (833) 630-2085.

Can a leaking roof be repaired, or does it need replacement?

Most leaks come from a local failure — flashing, a vent boot, a cracked joint — and can be repaired without replacing the roof. An inspection shows whether the damage is local or widespread.

How long does a typical repair take?

Focused repairs such as crack sealing, leak repair and flashing work are usually completed in a single visit once the problem area is identified.
